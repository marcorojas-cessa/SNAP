BASE FEATURES (focused mode, Distorted 3D Gaussian channel)
------------------------------------------------------------
- integrated_intensity: Sum of background-corrected signal in the local fit window.
- background: Estimated local background intensity near the candidate spot.
- snr: Signal-to-noise proxy (signal/background). Higher usually means cleaner spots.
- r_squared: Goodness-of-fit score for the model (closer to 1 is generally better).
- amplitude: Peak fitted intensity of the spot model.
- sigma_x: Fitted Gaussian width along x (spot spread in x).
- sigma_y: Fitted Gaussian width along y (spot spread in y).
- sigma_z: Fitted Gaussian width along z (axial spread).
- sigma_sum: Total spread proxy from sigma terms (larger means broader spot).
- sigma_product: Area/volume-style spread proxy from sigma product.
- sigma_xy_ratio: Ellipticity proxy from sigma_x/sigma_y (1 means symmetric in xy).
- sigma_z_ratio: Axial-to-lateral elongation proxy.
- amplitude_over_background: Peak contrast above background.
- rho_xy: Fit correlation term between x and y (distortion/coupling indicator).
- rho_xz: Fit correlation term between x and z.
- rho_yz: Fit correlation term between y and z.

CUSTOM EXPRESSIONS (scientific meaning)
---------------------------------------

Signal and Background (10)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- log_integrated_intensity: Stabilizes heavy-tailed spot intensity distributions.
- log_background: Compresses local background dynamic range.
- log_snr_proxy: Background-normalized signal proxy with log compression.
- poisson_snr_proxy: Poisson-like contrast proxy using signal+background variance scaling.
- shot_noise_proxy: Approximate Poisson-dominated contrast score.
- background_fraction: Fraction of local intensity explained by background.
- signal_fraction: Fraction of total local intensity assigned to spot signal.
- contrast_to_background: Classical local contrast ratio.
- background_subtracted_log_signal: Log-compressed signal after background subtraction.
- inverse_bg_penalty: Intensity penalized quadratically by background burden.

Fit Quality Couplings (9)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- quality_weighted_snr: Couples fit quality and normalized signal strength.
- quality_penalty: Penalizes bright but poorly fit detections.
- quality_bg_compound: High when both fit quality and background-normalized intensity are high.
- quality_vs_size_penalty: Rewards compact, high-quality fits.
- snr_vs_size_penalty: SNR penalized by broad spatial footprint.
- snr_quality_harmonic: Harmonic coupling of SNR and fit quality (penalizes imbalance).
- quality_bg_penalty: Penalizes otherwise good fits in high local background.
- quality_signal_fraction: Joint score for fit quality and local signal purity.
- quality_signal_harmonic_fraction: Harmonic coupling of fit quality and local signal-purity fraction.

Morphology and Compactness (15)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- ellipticity_log: Shape anisotropy magnitude in XY.
- ellipticity_ratio_xy: Raw XY sigma ratio (ellipticity).
- psf_roundness_penalty: Dimensionless PSF roundness deviation in XY.
- psf_roundness_score: Higher when lateral PSF widths are balanced.
- compactness_2d: Intensity concentration per estimated PSF area.
- size_penalized_snr_2d: Normalized signal penalized by broad PSF widths.
- quality_per_sigma_sum_2d: Fit quality normalized by spot footprint.
- quality_compactness_coupled_2d: 2D quality-intensity score penalized by spread and local background.
- intensity_over_sigma_product: Signal concentration using derived sigma-product.
- sigma_product_over_intensity: Inverse concentration measure; high for diffuse spots.
- sigma_sum_over_intensity: Diffuse-width penalty relative to signal.
- thompson_var_xy_proxy: Localization-variance proxy inspired by Thompson et al. (2002).
- thompson_precision_xy_proxy: Square-root localization precision proxy in XY.
- thompson_information_xy: Inverse XY precision proxy (higher implies better localization information).
- localization_information_density_xy: Localization-information proxy per lateral spread unit.

3D Anisotropy (9)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- compactness_3d: Intensity concentration per estimated PSF volume.
- z_anisotropy_abs: Axial-vs-lateral anisotropy magnitude.
- size_penalized_snr_3d: 3D normalized signal penalized by total width.
- sigma_z_compactness: Relative axial broadening.
- quality_anisotropy_3d: Quality penalized by excessive axial anisotropy.
- thompson_var_z_proxy: Axial localization-variance proxy inspired by Thompson-style scaling.
- thompson_precision_z_proxy: Square-root axial precision proxy.
- thompson_information_z: Inverse axial precision proxy.
- axial_to_lateral_precision_ratio: Relative axial-vs-lateral localization precision proxy.

Amplitude-centric (12)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- amp_bg_log: Log contrast of fitted amplitude against local background.
- log_amplitude: Log-compressed fitted amplitude.
- amp_to_intensity_ratio: Peak-to-total intensity ratio.
- amp_quality_coupled: High when amplitude contrast and fit quality are both strong.
- amplitude_subtracted_background: Background-corrected amplitude ratio.
- amplitude_shot_noise_ratio: Amplitude normalized by Poisson-like background scale.
- gaussian_energy_error_2d: Relative mismatch between integrated intensity and 2D Gaussian energy.
- gaussian_energy_ratio_2d: 2D Gaussian energy estimate normalized by integrated intensity.
- gaussian_energy_consistency_score_2d: Bounded 2D Gaussian energy-consistency score (higher is better).
- gaussian_energy_error_3d: Relative mismatch between integrated intensity and 3D Gaussian energy.
- gaussian_energy_ratio_3d: 3D Gaussian energy estimate normalized by integrated intensity.
- gaussian_energy_consistency_score_3d: Bounded 3D Gaussian energy-consistency score (higher is better).

Distortion and Correlation (6)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- distortion_energy: Correlation distortion energy for distorted Gaussian fits.
- distortion_penalized_quality: Fit quality penalized by strong axis correlations.
- distortion_magnitude: Root-energy distortion magnitude.
- distortion_weighted_snr: SNR proxy penalized by distortion terms.
- distortion_bg_coupled: Correlation distortion burden normalized by local signal.
- distortion_shape_coupled: Joint penalty for correlation distortion and axial elongation.

Advanced Model-Selection and Residual Stats (19)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
- info_gain_aic_norm: Normalized AIC evidence gain for Gaussian model.
- info_gain_bic_norm: Normalized BIC evidence gain for Gaussian model.
- lrt_stat_log: Log-likelihood-ratio evidence against flat model.
- poisson_advantage_norm: Normalized Poisson NLL advantage for Gaussian model.
- bic_posterior_like_weight: Sigmoid-transformed BIC evidence proxy for Gaussian-model preference.
- aic_posterior_like_weight: Sigmoid-transformed AIC evidence proxy for Gaussian-model preference.
- evidence_consensus_weight: Consensus evidence score requiring both AIC and BIC support.
- poisson_evidence_weight: Sigmoid-transformed Poisson likelihood evidence for Gaussian over flat.
- residual_robustness_ratio: Residual Gaussianity proxy via STD/MAD ratio.
- residual_shape_penalty: Residual non-normality penalty.
- residual_quality_joint: High for accurate fits with small residual relative to signal.
- model_evidence_quality: Couples fit quality to model-selection evidence magnitude.
- residual_normality_logp: Strength of residual non-normality evidence.
- evidence_residual_ratio: Model evidence normalized by residual scale.
- robust_bias_ratio: Residual bias normalized by robust dispersion.
- aic_bic_agreement: Agreement between AIC and BIC evidence signals.
- residual_gaussianity_score: Bounded residual-shape score favoring centered, near-Gaussian residuals.
- evidence_quality_consensus: Evidence-consensus score weighted by fit quality.
- model_evidence_residual_purity: Joint model-evidence score penalized by residual scale and bias.
